[Custom Characters / Skins Guide]
===================================
Icy Tower Wii supports custom characters and skins!

Included characters:
- fallguy: Fall Guy from Fall Guys (custom sprites & voice effects)
- impost: Impostor from Among Us (custom sprites & sounds)

How to add your own character skin:
1. Create a new folder inside sd:/apps/icytower/characters/ (e.g. MySkin).
2. Place your character's spritesheet or individual .bmp/.png frames inside it.
   - You can also provide custom sound effects (.wav / .ogg) and background music.
3. Configure the skin definition file (character.txt) with your frame names and sounds.
4. Launch the game, navigate to Game Options -> Character, and select your custom skin!
